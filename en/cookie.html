Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2025
This website does not use profiling or marketing cookies. We only use essential technical cookies and a font-related IP transfer via Google Fonts.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser during navigation. They can be used to make a site work, remember preferences, or collect analytics and marketing information.

Not all cookies are the same: some are essential for the website to function, while others require your explicit consent.

2. Cookies used on this website

This website only uses essential technical cookies. We do not use profiling, tracking, or marketing cookies of any kind.

Cookie / Technology Type Purpose Duration
cookie-notice-dismissed Technical Remembers that you dismissed the cookie information banner. 365 days
Google Fonts CDN Third party Loads web fonts. When loaded, your IP address is sent to Google. No browser cookie is set. Not applicable
Profiling cookies Not used — —

3. Google Fonts and IP transfer

This website loads fonts via Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When you visit this website, your browser sends a direct request to Google servers, automatically transmitting your IP address.

Google does not set cookies in your browser through this service, but the IP transfer is still considered personal data processing. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which provides safeguards for transfers to the United States.

Google LLC Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
Google Fonts FAQ: developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy

4. Contact form (Formspree)

The contact form on this website uses Formspree to send and manage messages. Formspree does not set cookies in your browser, but it processes the data you submit in the form (name, email, message) to deliver it to us.

Formspree, Inc. Privacy Policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

5. How to manage cookies

Because this website only uses essential technical cookies, your consent is not required for their use. You can still manage or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling technical cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Changes to this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated in the event of technical website changes or legal developments. The latest update date is always shown at the top of the page.
